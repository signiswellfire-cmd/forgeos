# RFC-0030 — Executive Dashboard

**RFC Number:** RFC-0030

**Title:** Executive Dashboard

**Status:** Approved

**Version:** 1.0.0

**Authors:** ForgeOS Architecture Office

**Last Updated:** 2026-08-01

---

# Abstract

This RFC defines the Executive Dashboard, the strategic command interface of ForgeOS.

The Executive Dashboard provides Founders, Executives, and organizational leaders with a comprehensive, explainable, and real-time view of organizational state. Rather than functioning as a traditional analytics dashboard, it serves as the operational command center for directing, evaluating, and evolving a Digital Organization.

The Executive Dashboard is the primary interface through which executive leadership observes organizational health, issues strategic direction, approves decisions, and measures long-term capability growth.

---

# Motivation

Most executive dashboards emphasize operational metrics such as:

* project completion;
* financial performance;
* issue counts;
* deployment frequency;
* employee utilization.

While valuable, these metrics fail to answer the fundamental executive question:

> **"Is the Organization becoming stronger?"**

ForgeOS therefore measures the Organization itself.

---

# Problem Statement

Without an integrated executive command interface:

* strategic visibility becomes fragmented;
* organizational health is difficult to assess;
* decisions lack supporting context;
* capability investment becomes reactive;
* governance becomes difficult to monitor.

ForgeOS requires a unified executive operating interface.

---

# Goals

The Executive Dashboard shall:

* provide strategic organizational visibility;
* support executive decision making;
* visualize organizational capability;
* monitor governance quality;
* identify organizational risks;
* remain implementation independent.

---

# Non-Goals

The Executive Dashboard does not define:

* user interface technology;
* visualization libraries;
* authentication;
* reporting software;
* mobile applications.

These concerns belong to implementation specifications.

---

# Decision

ForgeOS introduces the Executive Dashboard as the authoritative executive interface for organizational management.

Every dashboard view shall derive its information from governed organizational knowledge rather than disconnected operational systems.

The Dashboard presents organizational intelligence, not merely organizational data.

---

# Executive Principles

## Organization Before Projects

Projects are temporary.

Organizations are permanent.

The dashboard emphasizes organizational development.

---

## Capability Before Productivity

Long-term capability growth has greater strategic value than temporary productivity increases.

---

## Explainability Before Visualization

Every visualization must be traceable to authoritative organizational knowledge.

Executives should always understand why information is presented.

---

## Governance Before Automation

Automated recommendations support executive decisions.

They never replace executive authority.

---

## Continuous Awareness

The dashboard reflects the continuously evolving organizational state rather than static reporting periods.

---

# Dashboard Domains

The Executive Dashboard presents multiple strategic domains.

## Organization Overview

Displays:

* organizational maturity;
* active Missions;
* strategic objectives;
* executive priorities;
* capability summary.

---

## Mission Control

Displays:

* Mission portfolio;
* Mission health;
* Mission dependencies;
* Mission progress;
* validation status.

Mission information emphasizes organizational value rather than task completion.

---

## Workforce Intelligence

Displays:

* Professional distribution;
* competency coverage;
* capability availability;
* workload balance;
* succession risks.

The objective is workforce resilience.

---

## Organizational Health

Displays:

* Knowledge Health;
* Governance Health;
* Capability Health;
* Process Health;
* Architecture Health;
* Workforce Health.

Health trends are more important than individual point-in-time measurements.

---

## Knowledge Intelligence

Displays:

* promoted Knowledge Objects;
* Blueprint evolution;
* Engineering Memory growth;
* Executive Memory updates;
* knowledge quality indicators.

Knowledge is treated as a strategic organizational asset.

---

## Governance

Displays:

* pending approvals;
* architectural reviews;
* standards compliance;
* decision traceability;
* policy exceptions.

Governance visibility supports organizational quality.

---

## Risk Intelligence

Displays:

* architectural risks;
* capability gaps;
* governance weaknesses;
* operational concerns;
* strategic threats.

Risks should be prioritized according to organizational impact.

---

# Executive Actions

The Dashboard supports strategic actions including:

* creating Missions;
* approving Decisions;
* initiating Executive Meetings;
* reviewing organizational health;
* assigning strategic priorities;
* approving Blueprint promotion;
* launching improvement initiatives.

Actions remain subject to organizational governance.

---

# Executive Insights

The Dashboard generates explainable organizational insights.

Representative insights include:

* declining capability maturity;
* emerging architectural debt;
* insufficient competency coverage;
* repeated Mission failures;
* Blueprint reuse opportunities;
* organizational bottlenecks.

Every insight should reference supporting organizational evidence.

---

# Relationship to Executive Meetings

Executive Meetings consume Dashboard intelligence.

Meeting outcomes subsequently update Dashboard state.

This creates a continuous executive feedback loop.

---

# Relationship to Organization Health

Organization Health provides the strategic measurements displayed within the Dashboard.

The Dashboard visualizes organizational condition.

Organization Health defines how it is measured.

---

# Relationship to the Knowledge Graph

The Dashboard aggregates information from:

* Missions;
* Professionals;
* Capabilities;
* Knowledge Objects;
* Decisions;
* Blueprints;
* Engineering Memory;
* Executive Memory.

The Knowledge Graph provides the underlying organizational relationships.

---

# Relationship to the Context Builder

When executives request recommendations, the Context Builder assembles relevant strategic context before AI reasoning occurs.

This ensures recommendations remain grounded in organizational knowledge.

---

# Governance

Dashboard information must:

* remain explainable;
* reference authoritative sources;
* preserve traceability;
* reflect approved organizational knowledge.

The Dashboard shall never display unvalidated organizational conclusions as established facts.

---

# Alternatives Considered

## BI Dashboards

Rejected because traditional business intelligence focuses primarily on operational reporting rather than organizational evolution.

---

## Project Dashboards

Rejected because project status alone does not adequately represent organizational capability.

---

## AI Chat Interface

Rejected because conversational interfaces lack persistent organizational visibility and strategic situational awareness.

---

# Consequences

Positive outcomes include:

* unified executive visibility;
* stronger strategic governance;
* improved organizational planning;
* measurable capability evolution;
* explainable executive decisions;
* reduced Founder cognitive load.

Trade-offs include:

* increased organizational modeling;
* governance requirements;
* richer information architecture.

These trade-offs are accepted because they significantly improve executive effectiveness.
