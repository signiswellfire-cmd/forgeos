# RFC-0037 — Knowledge Recommendation Engine

**RFC Number:** RFC-0037

**Title:** Knowledge Recommendation Engine

**Status:** Approved

**Version:** 1.0.0

**Authors:** ForgeOS Architecture Office

**Last Updated:** 2026-08-01

---

# Abstract

This RFC defines the Knowledge Recommendation Engine (KRE), the subsystem responsible for proactively recommending organizational knowledge before it is explicitly requested.

While the Knowledge Query Engine retrieves knowledge in response to a query, the Knowledge Recommendation Engine continuously analyzes organizational context to identify information that should be surfaced to Professionals, Executives, and organizational subsystems.

The objective is to reduce knowledge discovery effort, prevent duplicated work, and strengthen organizational decision quality.

Recommendations are advisory and governed. They never override executive or professional judgment.

---

# Motivation

Professionals frequently do not know what organizational knowledge already exists.

Consequently:

* duplicate solutions are created;
* previous engineering experience is ignored;
* architectural consistency declines;
* Blueprints remain underutilized;
* valuable organizational knowledge is forgotten.

Organizations should not depend solely on manual discovery.

ForgeOS therefore recommends relevant knowledge proactively.

---

# Problem Statement

Reactive knowledge retrieval assumes users know what to search for.

In practice, organizational knowledge is often:

* unknown;
* forgotten;
* disconnected;
* hidden across multiple repositories.

ForgeOS requires a subsystem that continuously identifies relevant organizational knowledge based on context.

---

# Goals

The Knowledge Recommendation Engine shall:

* proactively recommend authoritative knowledge;
* reduce duplicate organizational effort;
* improve engineering consistency;
* strengthen Knowledge Promotion;
* remain explainable;
* remain implementation independent.

---

# Non-Goals

The Knowledge Recommendation Engine does not define:

* recommendation algorithms;
* machine learning models;
* personalization engines;
* notification systems;
* user interface behavior.

These belong to implementation specifications.

---

# Decision

ForgeOS introduces the Knowledge Recommendation Engine as the authoritative subsystem for proactive organizational knowledge discovery.

Recommendations shall be generated from governed organizational knowledge and supported by explainable evidence.

---

# Recommendation Principles

## Recommendation Before Reinvention

Existing validated organizational knowledge should be recommended before creating new knowledge.

---

## Authority Before Popularity

Recommendations prioritize organizational authority rather than usage frequency.

---

## Explainability Before Ranking

Every recommendation shall include:

* why it was recommended;
* supporting organizational evidence;
* authoritative references.

---

## Context Awareness

Recommendations shall consider organizational context rather than isolated user activity.

---

## Human Decision Authority

Recommendations assist Professionals and Executives.

Acceptance remains a human organizational decision.

---

# Recommendation Sources

Recommendations may originate from:

* active Missions;
* Executive Meetings;
* Organization Health;
* Engineering Memory;
* Executive Memory;
* Blueprints;
* Knowledge Objects;
* Engineering Standards;
* RFCs;
* Decisions;
* Strategic Objectives;
* Organizational Intelligence.

---

# Recommendation Categories

## Blueprint Recommendations

Suggest reusable Blueprints before creating new organizational solutions.

---

## Knowledge Recommendations

Surface existing organizational knowledge relevant to current work.

---

## Engineering Recommendations

Recommend engineering standards, implementation patterns, and architectural guidance.

---

## Mission Recommendations

Identify similar Missions, historical outcomes, reusable assets, and previous decisions.

---

## Workforce Recommendations

Suggest Professionals, Skills, Competencies, or Teams appropriate for organizational needs.

---

## Strategic Recommendations

Recommend organizational initiatives, capability investments, and governance improvements.

---

# Recommendation Pipeline

The conceptual recommendation pipeline is:

```text
Organizational Context
        ↓
Context Analysis
        ↓
Knowledge Graph Traversal
        ↓
Candidate Discovery
        ↓
Authority Evaluation
        ↓
Relevance Ranking
        ↓
Governance Validation
        ↓
Recommendation Package
```

---

# Recommendation Package

Every recommendation package contains:

* recommendation identifier;
* recommendation category;
* recommendation summary;
* organizational rationale;
* supporting evidence;
* confidence assessment;
* expected organizational benefit;
* authoritative references.

Recommendations should always be independently explainable.

---

# Context Signals

The engine may consider:

* Mission lifecycle;
* Professional responsibilities;
* Competency profile;
* active Blueprints;
* Executive priorities;
* Organization Health indicators;
* Engineering Memory;
* Decision history;
* strategic objectives.

Signals provide context.

They do not replace organizational governance.

---

# Recommendation Timing

Recommendations may occur:

* before Mission planning;
* during Mission execution;
* during architectural review;
* during Executive Meetings;
* during Knowledge Promotion;
* during strategic planning.

Recommendations should appear when organizational value is highest.

---

# Relationship to the Knowledge Query Engine

The Knowledge Query Engine responds to explicit questions.

The Knowledge Recommendation Engine identifies organizational knowledge before questions arise.

The Query Engine is reactive.

The Recommendation Engine is proactive.

---

# Relationship to the Organizational Intelligence Engine

The Organizational Intelligence Engine identifies strategic patterns.

The Knowledge Recommendation Engine translates relevant patterns into actionable knowledge recommendations.

Strategic reasoning supports recommendation quality.

---

# Relationship to the Context Builder

The Context Builder provides the organizational context used to generate recommendations.

The Recommendation Engine should never construct context independently.

---

# Relationship to the Knowledge Graph

The Knowledge Graph provides semantic relationships used to identify recommendation candidates.

Recommendations emerge from organizational relationships rather than isolated documents.

---

# Governance

Recommendations require:

* supporting evidence;
* authoritative references;
* explainable rationale;
* traceable provenance.

Recommendations without organizational justification shall not be presented.

---

# Alternatives Considered

## Document Suggestions

Rejected because document similarity alone does not represent organizational relevance.

---

## AI Chat Suggestions

Rejected because conversational recommendations are transient and insufficiently governed.

---

## Manual Knowledge Discovery

Rejected because organizational scale eventually exceeds manual discovery.

---

# Consequences

Positive outcomes include:

* increased Blueprint reuse;
* improved engineering consistency;
* reduced duplicated effort;
* stronger organizational learning;
* lower cognitive load;
* improved knowledge utilization.

Trade-offs include:

* recommendation governance;
* organizational metadata maintenance;
* relevance evaluation complexity.

These trade-offs are accepted because they substantially improve organizational effectiveness.
