# RFC-0031 — Organization Digital Twin

**RFC Number:** RFC-0031

**Title:** Organization Digital Twin

**Status:** Approved

**Version:** 1.0.0

**Authors:** ForgeOS Architecture Office

**Last Updated:** 2026-08-01

---

# Abstract

This RFC defines the Organization Digital Twin (ODT), a continuously synchronized virtual representation of a ForgeOS Organization.

The Organization Digital Twin models the Organization's current structure, capabilities, knowledge, governance, operational state, workforce composition, and strategic objectives. It enables ForgeOS to analyze, simulate, predict, and optimize organizational behavior before real-world decisions are executed.

The Digital Twin represents organizational reality—not merely organizational data.

---

# Motivation

Modern Digital Twins are widely used for manufacturing systems, buildings, supply chains, and industrial operations.

ForgeOS applies the same concept to engineering organizations.

Instead of simulating machines, ForgeOS simulates:

* organizational structure;
* workforce capability;
* knowledge maturity;
* mission execution;
* governance effectiveness;
* strategic evolution.

This enables evidence-based organizational decision making.

---

# Problem Statement

Organizations often make strategic decisions using incomplete or fragmented information.

Examples include:

* restructuring teams;
* adopting new technologies;
* prioritizing initiatives;
* expanding capabilities;
* reallocating workforce.

Without an integrated organizational model:

* strategic risk increases;
* unintended consequences remain hidden;
* organizational learning slows;
* executive confidence decreases.

ForgeOS therefore requires a complete organizational representation.

---

# Goals

The Organization Digital Twin shall:

* model the complete Organization;
* maintain synchronization with organizational state;
* support simulation;
* improve executive decision making;
* enable predictive analysis;
* remain implementation independent.

---

# Non-Goals

The Organization Digital Twin does not define:

* simulation algorithms;
* AI models;
* visualization technologies;
* persistence engines;
* synchronization protocols.

These belong to implementation specifications.

---

# Decision

ForgeOS introduces the Organization Digital Twin as the authoritative virtual representation of the Organization.

Every significant organizational entity shall be represented within the Digital Twin.

The Digital Twin becomes the strategic reasoning model for executive intelligence.

---

# Digital Twin Principles

## Organization-Centric

The Organization—not software systems—is the modeled entity.

---

## Continuous Synchronization

The Digital Twin reflects approved organizational state.

Synchronization occurs continuously as organizational knowledge evolves.

---

## Explainability

Every modeled relationship shall be traceable to authoritative organizational knowledge.

---

## Predictive Reasoning

The Digital Twin supports reasoning about possible futures without modifying organizational reality.

---

## Governance First

Simulation does not bypass organizational governance.

Recommendations remain subject to executive approval.

---

# Twin Scope

The Digital Twin models:

## Organizational Structure

* Executives
* Professionals
* Teams
* Departments
* Missions

---

## Organizational Knowledge

* Knowledge Objects
* Executive Memory
* Engineering Memory
* Decisions
* Blueprints

---

## Organizational Capability

* Skills
* Competencies
* Capabilities
* Workforce maturity

---

## Operational State

* Active Missions
* Processes
* Organizational Health
* Governance status

---

## Strategic State

* Vision
* Objectives
* Priorities
* Risks
* Opportunities

---

# Synchronization

The Digital Twin receives updates from:

* Mission Engine;
* Process Engine;
* Knowledge Promotion;
* Organization Health;
* Executive Meetings;
* Engineering Memory;
* Decision Records.

Synchronization preserves consistency between organizational reality and its virtual representation.

---

# Simulation

The Digital Twin enables simulation of:

* workforce changes;
* capability investments;
* organizational restructuring;
* Mission prioritization;
* governance modifications;
* Blueprint adoption;
* strategic alternatives.

Simulation never directly modifies organizational state.

---

# Scenario Analysis

Executives may evaluate scenarios including:

* adding new Professionals;
* removing organizational bottlenecks;
* improving competencies;
* reorganizing Teams;
* introducing automation;
* adopting new Blueprints.

Scenario outcomes remain advisory until approved.

---

# Relationship to the Knowledge Graph

The Knowledge Graph provides the semantic relationships represented within the Digital Twin.

The Digital Twin extends those relationships into a continuously synchronized organizational model.

---

# Relationship to Executive Dashboard

The Executive Dashboard visualizes the current state of the Digital Twin.

Strategic dashboards derive information from the Digital Twin rather than independently aggregating data.

---

# Relationship to Organization Health

Organization Health provides measurable indicators describing the condition of the Digital Twin.

Health metrics become properties of the virtual Organization.

---

# Governance

The Digital Twin represents approved organizational knowledge only.

Speculative or unvalidated information shall not become part of the authoritative organizational model.

---

# Alternatives Considered

## Static Organization Charts

Rejected because organizational relationships evolve continuously.

---

## Analytics Dashboards

Rejected because dashboards observe organizational state but do not model it.

---

## AI Memory

Rejected because memory alone cannot represent organizational structure or simulate future organizational states.

---

# Consequences

Positive outcomes include:

* unified organizational representation;
* predictive organizational reasoning;
* stronger executive planning;
* improved simulation;
* enhanced organizational explainability;
* reduced strategic risk.

Trade-offs include:

* synchronization complexity;
* governance requirements;
* richer organizational modeling.

These trade-offs are accepted because they significantly improve long-term organizational intelligence.
