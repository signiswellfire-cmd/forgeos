# ForgeOS Conceptual Model

## Purpose

This document defines the conceptual world of ForgeOS.

It intentionally does NOT define implementation,
architecture, technologies, or engineering decisions.

Instead it defines how ForgeOS should be understood as
a living digital software company.

---

# Core Philosophy

ForgeOS is a digital software company that resides on
its owner's computer.

The owner does not operate AI tools.

The owner leads a company.

---

# Core Concepts

## User

Defines vision.

Owns projects.

Communicates exclusively with Advisors.

---

## Project

A temporary initiative to transform an idea into a
working product.

Each project has exactly one Project Owner.

---

## Advisor

The trusted representative between the user and the
Organization.

Responsibilities:

- Discover vision.
- Challenge assumptions.
- Build interactive blueprints.
- Manage execution.
- Recommend decisions.
- Protect quality.
- Represent the Organization.
- Own project success.

The Advisor always recommends what is best for the
project, even when it differs from the user's initial
request.

---

## Organization

The digital software company responsible for delivering
the project.

It contains Digital Professionals.

The Organization persists across projects and becomes
more capable over time.

---

## Digital Professionals

Digital employees with:

- Name
- Profession
- Experience
- Achievements
- Personal Knowledge
- Specializations
- Reputation
- Professional Development history

They are employees, not AI agents.

---

## ForgeOS Academy (FOSA)

The institutional memory of the Organization.

Contains curated organizational knowledge.

Digital Professionals learn from FOSA.

They also contribute candidate knowledge through
structured review.

---

## Master ForgeOS Academy

Maintained only by ForgeOS HQ.

Receives candidate knowledge from customer ForgeOS
installations with explicit user consent.

Knowledge is reviewed, researched, curated, versioned,
and distributed through Academy updates and Experience
Packs.

---

## Professional Development

Digital Professionals may undergo optional professional
development through ForgeOS HQ.

Training is initiated only with the customer's explicit
approval and may be a paid service.

The customer receives an improved version of the Digital
Professional while retaining control over their local
organization.

---

## Blueprint

Before implementation begins, the Advisor creates an
interactive simulation of the proposed product.

The Blueprint is approved through experience rather than
documents.

Only an approved Blueprint may enter implementation.

---

## Organizational Learning

Projects generate:

- achievements,
- candidate knowledge,
- reusable experience.

The Organization continuously improves while maintaining
professional governance over permanent knowledge.

---

## Project Completion

Success is not measured by completed tasks.

Success is measured by delivering a product that the
Project Owner genuinely wants to use.

---

## Communication Principle

Users communicate only with Advisors.

Digital Professionals never communicate directly with
users.

---

## Governance Principle

Each project has exactly one Project Owner.

Each ForgeOS installation has exactly one Superadmin.

Only the Project Owner directs the Advisor.

The Superadmin governs the ForgeOS installation.

---

## North Star

ForgeOS exists to transform ideas into professionally
delivered software through a living digital organization
rather than isolated AI interactions.
