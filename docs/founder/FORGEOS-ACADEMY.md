# ForgeOS Academy (FOSA)

## Purpose

This document defines the philosophy, governance, and lifecycle of ForgeOS Academy (FOSA).

ForgeOS Academy is not a database.

It is the institutional memory of the Organization.

It preserves, curates, and continuously improves the collective knowledge accumulated through every project, every Digital Professional, and every approved organizational learning experience.

---

# Philosophy

Organizations become stronger when they remember.

ForgeOS Academy exists so that valuable knowledge is never lost.

Instead of repeatedly solving the same problems, Digital Professionals continuously learn from the accumulated experience of the Organization.

Knowledge is treated as a strategic organizational asset.

---

# Two Levels of ForgeOS Academy

ForgeOS consists of two independent Academy environments.

## Local ForgeOS Academy

Every ForgeOS installation contains its own Local Academy.

The Local Academy stores:

- organization-specific knowledge,
- project knowledge,
- company standards,
- reusable workflows,
- local best practices,
- approved organizational experience.

The Local Academy belongs entirely to the customer.

---

## Master ForgeOS Academy

The Master Academy exists only within ForgeOS HQ.

It contains:

- curated industry knowledge,
- engineering best practices,
- organizational standards,
- research outcomes,
- reusable experience,
- Experience Packs,
- organizational improvements.

The Master Academy is continuously improved through research and knowledge governance.

---

# Candidate Knowledge

Projects continuously generate new experience.

Not every experience should become permanent organizational knowledge.

Instead, Digital Professionals and Advisors may recommend Candidate Knowledge after project completion.

Candidate Knowledge is a proposal.

It is not automatically accepted into ForgeOS Academy.

---

# Knowledge Governance

All permanent knowledge is governed.

Before entering ForgeOS Academy, every Candidate Knowledge item must be:

- reviewed,
- researched,
- validated,
- compared with existing knowledge,
- categorized,
- versioned,
- approved.

Knowledge quality is always more important than knowledge quantity.

---

# Knowledge Management Department

ForgeOS HQ contains a dedicated Knowledge Management Department responsible for maintaining the Master Academy.

The department may include roles such as:

- Chief Knowledge Officer
- Knowledge Domain Architect
- Knowledge Researcher
- Knowledge Curator
- Knowledge Publisher

Each role contributes to preserving the quality and integrity of the Academy.

---

# Knowledge Research

Knowledge submissions are not accepted blindly.

Knowledge Researchers investigate:

- technical correctness,
- alternative solutions,
- industry practices,
- long-term maintainability,
- practical effectiveness.

Where appropriate, they improve the proposed knowledge before recommending it for inclusion.

---

# Knowledge Curation

Knowledge Curators ensure that ForgeOS Academy remains:

- organized,
- consistent,
- searchable,
- versioned,
- free from duplication,
- free from obsolete practices.

The Academy should grow through curation rather than accumulation.

---

# Knowledge Publication

Approved knowledge may be distributed as:

- Academy Updates,
- Experience Packs,
- Industry Packs,
- Professional Development material,
- organizational improvements.

Knowledge becomes reusable across the ForgeOS ecosystem.

---

# Professional Development

ForgeOS Academy supports Workforce Development.

Digital Professionals may study organizational knowledge to expand their expertise.

Advanced Professional Development may be provided by ForgeOS HQ as an optional service.

Professional Development is always initiated with explicit customer approval.

---

# Customer Contributions

Customers may choose to contribute Candidate Knowledge to ForgeOS HQ.

Submission is always voluntary.

Only knowledge approved by the customer may leave the local ForgeOS installation.

Private business information, confidential data, and customer ownership must always be respected.

---

---

# Relationship with Digital Professionals

Digital Professionals:

- consult ForgeOS Academy,
- study organizational knowledge,
- recommend Candidate Knowledge,
- improve through Professional Development.

ForgeOS Academy strengthens Digital Professionals without replacing their personal experience.

---

# Relationship with ForgeOS HQ

ForgeOS HQ is responsible for maintaining the Master Academy.

It continuously researches, validates, and improves organizational knowledge before distributing improvements back to the ForgeOS ecosystem.

ForgeOS HQ exists to strengthen every participating ForgeOS installation while respecting customer ownership and privacy.

---

# Guiding Principles

ForgeOS Academy should always be:

- accurate,
- trustworthy,
- professionally curated,
- continuously improving,
- evidence-based,
- organizationally valuable.

Knowledge should never become permanent merely because it exists.

Knowledge earns its place through quality, validation, and long-term usefulness.

---

# Definition of Success

ForgeOS Academy is successful when:

- valuable knowledge is never lost,
- Digital Professionals become more capable,
- projects benefit from previous experience,
- organizational learning compounds over time,
- every ForgeOS installation becomes stronger while preserving customer ownership and trust.
